# Applications

Everything Flux deploys, grouped by namespace. Each name links to its
directory in the repository. An app is enabled when its `ks.yaml` is listed in
the namespace `kustomization.yaml`.

Namespaces are split into two groups, and a namespace's group applies to every
app in it:

- **Platform**: what runs, connects, secures, stores, backs up, upgrades or
  observes the cluster, plus shared backing services such as databases that
  other apps depend on.
- **Workloads**: apps used for their own sake, where nothing else in the
  cluster depends on them.

The columns:

- **Exposure**: `external` when the app has a route on `envoy-external`
  (reachable from the internet), `internal` when it only has a route on
  `envoy-internal` (LAN and tailnet), `—` when it has no route.
- **Uses**: the [components](components.md) its Flux Kustomization opts into:
  PVC [backup](components.md#kopiurbackup), its own
  [postgres](components.md#postgres) or [dragonfly](components.md#dragonfly),
  the [gpu](components.md#gpu), and
  [scale-to-zero](components.md#zeroscalernfs) when NFS on the NAS stops
  answering.
- **Upstream**: the project the app is built from.

The tables are generated by `just docs-apps` from `kubernetes/apps`. Only
Purpose and Upstream are written by hand; the generator keeps them, adds new
apps with a `TODO` purpose, and a pre-commit hook fails until the page is up
to date and every app has a purpose.
